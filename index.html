everything you should learn about cyber attacks

Cyber attacks involve unauthorized actions targeting computer systems, networks, or digital infrastructure. Their goals include stealing information, altering data, disabling systems, or causing operational disruption. Rising digital dependence and interconnected systems increase vulnerability.

how to avoid it


To avoid cyber attacks, use multi factor authentication, strong passwords, regular software updates, employee training, and advanced security tools such as firewalls and encryption. These measures significantly reduce risks by closing common vulnerabilities and preventing unauthorized access.
